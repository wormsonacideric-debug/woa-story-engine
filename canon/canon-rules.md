# WOA Canon Rules

## Authority and source hierarchy
- Explicit user decisions and current approved WOA source documents outrank summaries.
- The six website character images for Eric, Manni, Nigel, Peter, Rufus and Tappy are explicitly approved as canonical identity artwork by the user (2026-11-11). They are not merely style references.
- The three website location images for The High Scraparral, Manchester and The Festivals are accepted for the initial world reference set.
- The original artwork register remains useful for character-owned attributes, anatomy constraints, provenance and historical canon. Where it refers to older artwork for one of the six characters, the user's explicit approval of the website image governs the initial engine reference.
- Arthur, Edwin and Brian are known characters, but their visual references are not yet imported. Do not generate or substitute their appearances.

## Visual canon
- Canonical artwork is the source of truth for character appearance, proportions, markings, colours, accessories and style.
- Every generation task must use a REFERENCE BINDING manifest listing exact approved assets.
- Never reconstruct a canonical character from memory, prose or a previous generated image.
- Preserve the bound source appearance. Stop if references are unclear or contradictory.
- Canonical source files are immutable. New approved versions receive new IDs and provenance.

## Character constraints
- Eric cannot hold objects.
- Peter is an Early Bird character; do not infer additional traits from this label.
- Preserve character-specific anatomy and attribute ownership. Manni must not become worm anatomy; Rufus remains a shark despite identifying as a dog; Nigel's hat and Rufus's checked cap must not migrate to other characters.
- Use the scene record to determine who is present.

## World constraints
- The High Scraparral, Manchester and The Festivals have accepted initial location artwork.
- Do not invent location layout, signage, geography, period objects or background characters beyond approved source material.
- WOA has no assumed home location. Do not use Silves as WOA geography or imagery.

## Narrative canon
- Actual author chronology and audience reveal order are separate data sets.
- Character knowledge is not author knowledge. Do not give characters the full origin story before it is revealed to them.
- The Thing We Don't Talk About remains a recurring mystery; do not over-explain it.
- Do not invent dialogue, events, motivations, relationships, dates or transitions.
- Do not move a character or object between scenes without a recorded story event.

## Generation constraints
- One scene / one requested frame at a time unless explicitly asked for a sequence.
- No extra props, captions, speech bubbles, logos, watermarks or background detail unless specified.
- A rejected candidate must never become a canonical reference.
- Evaluation scores are evidence, not proof. Human approval is mandatory.

## Conflict protocol
Identify conflicting records, explain the consequence, stop production, ask the human editor to resolve the conflict, and record the resolution as a dated decision.
