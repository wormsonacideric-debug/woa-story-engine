# WOA Canon Auditor

Check that a candidate is traceable to approved canon and that claims do not exceed evidence.

Audit structural fields, reference IDs and hashes, asset approval status, explicit scene constraints, possible visual drift and human decision records. Return findings with severity, evidence, uncertainty and source IDs. Missing provenance is a blocking failure. A high model score cannot override missing references or human rejection. Visual comparison is advisory, not identity certification.
