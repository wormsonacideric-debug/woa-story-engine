# WOA Story Bible

## Status
Imported working canon. Source: `WOA_Story_Universe_Baseline_v1.0.docx`, supplemented by the 17-chapter storyboard outline. This index does not supersede source documents.

## Core premise
WORMS ON ACID is an absurdist story universe about altered creatures trying to understand who they are, what happened and how they came to be together. The central recurring mystery is **The Thing We Don't Talk About**.

## Two levels of truth
- **Character-level truth:** confused or missing memories, instinctive trust, fragments and unexplained memories. Characters do not properly remember the central event.
- **Author-level truth:** Botanists -> hyperspace incident -> biological matter displaced/scrambled -> transformed beings -> crop-circle awakening -> gang formation -> music festival -> heavy night -> High Scraparral/scrapyard awakening.

These layers must never be collapsed. See `story/knowledge-ledger.json`.

## Narrative structures
- Five-chapter opening: The Morning After; Manchester; The Crew; The Music; Onwards.
- Larger 17-chapter circular mystery arc: `story/chapter-registry.json`.
- Actual event order and audience reveal order are separate in `story/timeline.json`.
- The loop is narrative, not a time loop.

## Canon sources
- Character identity: approved asset IDs in `canon/artwork-registry.json` and per-character records.
- Location identity: artwork registry and per-location records.
- Scene detail: approved scene records only.
- Character knowledge: `story/knowledge-ledger.json` and scene-by-scene updates.

## Unresolved material
The Botanists' ultimate purpose, selection of entities, discorporeal entities, exact transformation mechanisms, surviving memories, Nigel's seaside instinct, Rufus's dog identity, High Scraparral's origin and the heavy night remain deliberately unexplained.

## Current limits
Chapters 6–17 are outline-level, not complete scripts. Arthur, Edwin and Brian are known characters but their image references are not imported. Peter's only confirmed narrative designation is Early Bird.
